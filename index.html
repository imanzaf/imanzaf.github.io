About

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer in AI Research & Development at NatWest Group, where I design production LLM frameworks for high-stakes applications in financial services. Alongside this I'm completing an MSc in Banking and Digital Finance at UCL, working on a dissertation on whether deployment-realistic financial LLM agents deceive under goal conflict — and how to detect it. What draws me in is the gap between benchmark numbers and how systems actually behave once they're live.
